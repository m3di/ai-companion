# Decisions — ideas measured

Every idea that reached a decision, with its verdict: accepted ones name the
invariant they serve, the milestone they advance, what they delete and how we
will know they worked; rejected ones keep their reason. The idea test that
produces these rows is in the [Charter](CHARTER.md#how-to-work-against-this).

`npx tsx src/proposalCli.ts accept|reject` appends a row here mechanically
(`src/proposals.ts`); rows decided in a session are added by hand. Newest last.
